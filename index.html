DAMMEN

International Draughts — 10×10 — Official FMJD rules
White to move
⚪ White: 20 ⚫ Black: 20
Official rules
  • 10×10 board, 20 pieces per side on the dark squares. White moves first.
  • Men move one square diagonally forward.
  • Men capture by jumping diagonally forward or backward (eating backwards is allowed).
  • Capturing is mandatory, and you must play the sequence that captures the maximum number of pieces (majority rule).
  • Multi-jumps continue from the landing square; captured pieces are removed only after the whole sequence ends, and the same piece cannot be jumped twice.
  • A man promotes to a king (dame) only when its move ends on the far row — passing through mid-capture does not promote.
  • Kings are flying: they move any distance along a diagonal, capture a piece at any distance, and may land on any empty square beyond it.
  • You lose when you have no legal move (no pieces left, or all blocked).
